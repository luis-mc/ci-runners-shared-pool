§ 1What and why

The owner asked what other self-hosted runner pools do and which of their ideas would make crsp the best runner pool for your own machines. Fifteen projects were studied, from single-machine tools to fleet managers and paid services. This page lists every finding, how well it was checked, how much it is worth to crsp, and where it stands. Other projects are not named here; crsp takes ideas only and keeps its own code.

Now: 52 findings: 30 To do, 11 Live, 8 Running, 3 Needs you.

How to read it. How it was checked: Checked (docs) means confirmed in the other tools' own published docs or in GitHub's docs; Checked (code) means confirmed in crsp's own specs and code; Reported means seen once and not confirmed. Value: High, Medium or Low for a pool of your own PCs that exists to save you money. Status uses the same words as Full Progress: To do, Running, Waiting, Needs you, Built, Live. Full Progress holds the live status of each task; this page is updated at each check-in.

§ 3Rules the findings follow

  • Never cost the user money by default. Anything that can use paid GitHub-hosted minutes is opt-in, per repository, with a cap (D52).
  • Protect the PC first. A job waits when the PC's load is unknown (D53), when it runs on battery, or when the user pauses it.
  • Own code only. Ideas from other projects are rewritten as crsp's own behaviour; no code is copied.
  • Every finding is a requirement. Each one is written into its feature spec and gets a task, so it is built in its milestone, not forgotten.
