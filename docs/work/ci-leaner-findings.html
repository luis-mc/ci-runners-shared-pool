Leaner CI: findings and solutions

ci-runners-shared-pool, analysed 26 Nov 2026 from the last 200 workflow runs and the job and step timings of recent full runs. Goal: spend less CI time without lowering what a pull request proves. Version 1, written by the project session; the dev-standards session reviews it in section 7, then we decide which fixes belong in dev-standards (every project benefits) and which stay here.

1. The numbers behind it

76%of workflow wall time is the CI workflow (1 409 of 1 855 min, last 200 runs)
58%of 60 CI runs were not green: 23 failed, 12 cancelled
25–28 minwall time of a full run; the base image alone is 21 min
2–4 srunner queue wait: not a problem
5 of 12pushes to main ran the whole suite (17–46 min)
~105 MBof binaries uploaded per run (6 targets, 16–19 MB each)

Wall minutes per workflow, last 200 runs: CI 1 409 · Release 97 · Tray smoke 89 · Installer packages 79 · Package 64 · Installer 61 · dev-standards 53 · Train cleanup 3.

Checked and fine

  • Runner queue wait: 2–4 s per job on GitHub-hosted runners. No queue problem today.
  • Cache hit rates: pnpm 8 of 8, Go (Linux) 7 of 7, Go (macOS, Windows) hit, Playwright browser 4 of 4, golangci-lint cache hit.
  • Double runs: ci.yml triggers on pull_request and on push to main only, so a PR branch does not run twice. dev-standards behaves the same.
  • dev-standards workflow: 51 runs, 53 min in total, 17 failed on policy checks (R1, T2, guards); cheap.
  • Unverified: Docker layer cache for the base image (21 min); whether arm64 image is needed on every PR; minute costs split between hosted and self-hosted runners.

Answers to section 6
  1. Yes. ci_scope.py (DL-80: full, ci-smoke, rust-release, affected) and area_select.py with ci-areas.json (DS9: areas to jobs; unknown path means all) are shipped and tested in tools/test_ci_speedups.py. pass_records.py (DL20) skips a shard whose inputs already passed. Key the image on a hash of all its inputs plus the base image digest, pin every FROM by digest, pull on a key hit, rebuild on a schedule with --pull --no-cache, and rebuild when a pinned digest changes (Docker best practices).
